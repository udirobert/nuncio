The workforce

Agents do the work. Humans get the meeting.

Every role at nuncio is performed by an agent — research, representation, and dispatch — with Masky avatars as their faces and voices. The human sender stays in charge through a written playbook; the human recipient is always told they're talking to an AI.

Research

Scout

Researches each recipient from public sources — role, company, recent posts — and distills honest personalization hooks. Low-confidence research blocks the send rather than shipping a flimsy message.

Representation · Masky avatar

Emissary

The live representative on every conversation link — a Masky avatar that answers questions on camera, guided by the sender's playbook, disclosed from the first second. Fallbacks: Synthesia (LiveKit) and Anam.

Dispatch

Concierge

Delivers the first touch, watches live sessions for booking intent, and hands warm prospects to the human — an explicit, expiring call request or a scheduling link. Never promises availability the sender didn't declare.

How it works

One pipeline, four agents' worth of work.

1

Research

Scout enriches the recipient from public sources and scores research confidence before anything is sent.

2

Playbook

The sender's wants, offer, wiggle room, and hard constraints become the twin's constitution — sanitized against injection before it reaches any model.

3

Represent

Emissary holds the first conversation on a live link — Masky avatar, governed prompt, booking-aware. Recorded video is the fallback artifact.

4

Hand off

Concierge escalates warm conversations to the human: a scoped call request inside the same room, or the sender's booking link. Consent gates everything.

Communities & wellbeing

Presence without the performance.

"The artifact doesn't advertise the conversation — it is the conversation."

Honesty as the interface

Every nuncio touch is labeled "AI representative — disclosed to your recipient." No disguised volume, no fake "I recorded this just for you." Trust is the product: recipients who know they're talking to an agent still engage, because the engagement is real.

Reconnect mode

Beyond sales: nuncio's reconnect mode helps people restart lapsed relationships — a card built around a real shared memory, followed by a live representative that carries the conversation until both humans are ready to talk. Presence, not pressure.

Live now

Masky avatar provider integrated end-to-end (masky-embed transport): session creation, governed per-turn replies via /api/live/turn, and the embedded live view. LiveKit/Synthesia and Anam paths already in production. Credit-gated, rate-limited, metrics-instrumented.

Deliberately not claimed

Masky turns render in seconds-to-a-minute rather than streaming — it's a considered reply, not a phone call. Human call bridging is opt-in and expiry-bound. We say so on the page.
