# Starter prompts — ChatGPT directory (nuncio)

Use these as **Example Prompts** on the listing (user language, not marketing).

1. write my first message to this prospect  
2. research https://linkedin.com/in/… and draft a LinkedIn DM I can paste  
3. draft a short cold email to this founder from their profile URL  
4. give me a WhatsApp first-touch based on this public profile  
5. (demo) show a sample research + draft without hitting live research  

Avoid pricing, trials, or “better than X” in listing copy. Point paid render/live link to the product site / existing account only.
